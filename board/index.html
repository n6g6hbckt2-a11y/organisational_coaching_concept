Board & NED

Independent judgement on risk, capital and people.

We take non-executive and committee roles, and advise Chairs and investor boards directly. We are based in the UK and Switzerland, and work across London, East Anglia, the Channel Islands and Swiss markets.

Where we add most

Regulated financial services

Specialist lenders, challenger banks, building societies, wealth managers, and fund and trust administrators in the Channel Islands, Malta and Switzerland. Boards that need treasury, balance-sheet and regulatory fluency, not just general oversight.

Investor-backed growth businesses

Private equity and family-office portfolio companies preparing to scale, refinance or exit. Founder–investor alignment, finance function readiness, and building a board that can hold management to account.

Science, life-sciences and deep-tech

University spin-outs and growing science companies, particularly around Cambridge and Norwich, where a scientifically literate accountant and a capital-markets banker can help technical founders become a commercial board.

UK–Swiss cross-border businesses

Groups operating across both jurisdictions that need someone who understands both regulatory cultures, the corporate structures between them and the commercial relationships that make them work.

How we sit at the table

Compared to what?

Every option a board considers has a cost, and the cost of the alternative is usually left out. We put it back in. We are comfortable being the person who asks the uncomfortable question, and equally comfortable supporting management once the decision is made.
