The Principals

One has run the balance sheet. One has audited it.

James and Kathryn Woodeson spent their first careers inside banking, audit and consulting before turning to advise boards and leadership teams. Every engagement is led by one or both of them.

James Woodeson

Co-founder · Capital markets · Board advisory

James spent 15 years at HSBC in roles around the world, rising to Managing Director with responsibility for £10bn+ of multi-currency balance sheets and $80bn of debt issuance. He then led the commercial side of the British-Swiss Chamber of Commerce and founded and grew his own business. He now advises Chairs, CEOs and investor-backed founders on the point where capital decisions meet people decisions.

  • Banking: former Managing Director, HSBC. Balance-sheet management, debt capital markets, P&L leadership across geographies
  • Commercial: former Secretary General (Acting Commercial Director), British-Swiss Chamber of Commerce
  • Enterprise: founder; business built to £2.5m revenue
  • Governance: Non-Executive Director
  • Method: systemic team and organisational practice (Gestalt, GPO; ICF PCC); 4,000+ hours with senior leaders
  • Education: BSc Banking & Finance

Kathryn Woodeson

Co-founder · Financial integrity · Regulatory risk

Kathryn trained as a Chartered Accountant at KPMG, then worked as a management consultant with two Big 4 firms, as a Senior Manager at Deloitte and in in-house Head of Change roles in financial services. Before finance she was a research chemist. She has worked with more than 40 companies across five countries, and advises boards and executives on cross-border structure, regulatory risk and the leadership of change.

  • Audit & finance: Chartered Accountant (ACA / FCA), KPMG; Senior Manager, Deloitte
  • Regulation: UK/Swiss corporate structuring and regulatory risk (FINMA, MFSA, JFSC)
  • Change: Head of Change roles in financial services; 40+ companies in five countries
  • Science: 1st Class MSci Chemistry, Bristol; former R&D researcher, DuPont
  • Method: executive and team practice (ICF PCC); 1,000+ hours with C-suite leaders in technology, pharma and FMCG

Why two

Different instincts, same table.

James reads a business from the capital side: funding, risk appetite, what the lenders and investors will tolerate. Kathryn reads it from the control side: whether the numbers are true, the structure is sound and the regulator will be comfortable. Both read the room.

For larger, multi-country mandates we bring in senior practitioners we know and trust, chosen for the brief and directed by us.
