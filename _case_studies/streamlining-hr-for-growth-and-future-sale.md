<h2>Business challenge</h2>
A recently acquired online retail business, with strong sales over the past two years, aimed to develop and grow further for potential sale within 3-5 years. To enhance consistency, compliance, and efficiency and boost market value, the business was looking to replace its limited and largely paper-based HR processes with an HR system which would integrate with a time management system.
<h2>Our collaboration</h2>
We worked with the business to establish what elements were essential, those that were desirable and the available spend. We did a market review, sourced different options and presented back the best options to the leadership team.
<p>What we learned:</p><p>Changes in processes or ways of working can face resistance across the whole organisation, However, this can be mitigated by securing stakeholder buy-in and involving diverse user profiles from the beginning, allowing them to contribute to the design. Effective training and ongoing communication are crucial to ensuring people feel supported through the change and understand the benefits for themselves.</p>
<h2>Benefits</h2>
<p>The selected HRIS, though simple and relatively inexpensive, met the business’s immediate needs. It improved efficiency and compliance, while also providing the added benefit of enabling the business to use HR data and insights for decision-making for the first time.</p>
