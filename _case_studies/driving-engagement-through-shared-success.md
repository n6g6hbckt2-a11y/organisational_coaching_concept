<h2>Business challenge</h2>
Following employee engagement sessions it was evident that employees felt their efforts were not always recognised, even though they worked hard, often going above and beyond. Despite this, only managers benefited through the annual bonus scheme which created a level of animosity. It was therefore agreed that the business should implement a scheme that would allow all employees to share in the success.
<h2>Our collaboration</h2>
We collaborated with the business to identify feasible parameters and then conducted market research to identify suitable partners. A proposal was then put to the Executive Committee for approval which included recommended targets and potential payout levels with modelled costings. To encourage uptake of the scheme, we designed and conducted multiple roadshows across various sites to explain the scheme’s mechanics, benefits, and to address any questions. We coordinated with multiple internal stakeholders, including branding, communications, and finance, to ensure the scheme’s success.
<p>What we learned:</p><p>This was a great example of where employee feedback had been heard and acted upon. It was important to remind employees of this, as it can often be overlooked. Additionally, the investment of time in the roadshow and direct employee engagement proved to be hugely beneficial on the uptake of the scheme. This was demonstrated by the higher-than-average take-up rate of the scheme, compared to other businesses with similar schemes.</p>
<h2>Benefits</h2>
<ul><li>The scheme was designed so that employees had personally invested, giving them skin in the game, unlike a standard bonus scheme. This led to increased focus and awareness of business performance outcomes.</li><li>The take-up of the scheme was 27% against an average of 20% for a scheme in its first year demonstrating the success of the communication and engagement strategy.</li></ul>
