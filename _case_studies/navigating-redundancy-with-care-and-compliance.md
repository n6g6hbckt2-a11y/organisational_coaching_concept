<h2>Business challenge</h2>
<p>The business faced falling revenues and cash flow problems. To survive, it needed to cut overhead costs and concentrate on profitable lines. This required a quick and significant reduction in headcount.</p>
<h2>Our collaboration</h2>
<p>The business was unfamiliar with the redundancy process. Initially, we briefed the CEO and Senior Leadership Team about the process, clarified the key messages to communicate, and agreed on timeframes.</p><p>As many managers were experiencing redundancy for the first time, we arranged training sessions to explain the purpose and conduct of consultation meetings. This was a hand-holding exercise to ensure legal compliance and support for those leaving the business due to redundancy, ensuring they felt supported and left on good terms.</p>
<p>What we learned:</p><p>Redundancy and restructures can indeed be overwhelming, especially for a manager facing them for the first time. Clear communication, empathy, and strong support can help them navigate these challenges confidently. Providing guidance on effective planning, emotional resilience, and maintaining team morale is essential.</p>
<h2>Benefits</h2>
<ul><li>The business met all legal obligations and swiftly reduced overhead costs, enhancing its survival prospects.</li><li>Both managers and employees felt well-supported throughout the process, describing it as professional and well-organised. This was particularly important for departing employees, as it left a lasting positive impression of the company.</li></ul>
