<h2>Client&#x27;s challenge</h2>
The organisation offers coaching for specific situations from an internal pool of coaches, who work predominantly 1:1 with a range of people with different seniority from across their locations. They also offer group and team coaching, which is a new initiative where the coach pool are developing their skills.
<h2>Our collaboration</h2>
<p>We were asked if we could provide a new supervisor for the group, who had worked with another provider for some time. There had been some concerns expressed about the style of supervision which was taking place.</p><p>During a chemistry session between the team and our supervisor, we sought to understand what was needed by the individuals and the collective. We explored how we might form a working alliance focussed on their personal and collective development needs.</p><p>The team was very experienced in their 1:1 coaching and as a result, they realised how beneficial it would be for an objective outsider to support the ongoing development of this team.</p><p>We were able to provide 1:1 and Group supervision and also, a bespoke Group Coaching training program.</p><p>The supervision also included a Masterclass on Creative Coaching, which was a topic which really mattered to the team.</p>
<p>What we learned:</p><p>A group of coaches has a variety of different development needs and there are often common themes. The nuances lend themselves to deeper development within a group setting. Supporting this with 1:1 sessions is transformational.</p>
<h2>Benefits</h2>
<p>The client was able to offer their team of coaches a qualified Supervisor to work with them in a variety of ways. As this started during and after the lockdown periods of Covid, we were also able to offer both virtual and in-person development sessions.</p><p>As the needs of the group were not limited to supervision, we were able to provide a different coach to support their development as group coaches, through a training solution.</p><p>Our relational approach also supported the development of the identity of this group of experts to become a team. Learning from and with each other.</p>
