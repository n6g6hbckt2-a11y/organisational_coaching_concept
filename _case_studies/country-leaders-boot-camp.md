<h2>Business challenge</h2>
<p>The Country Leader role, with responsibility and oversight of all in-country activities, presents a significant challenge for what many past and present leaders describe as one of the most rewarding roles they have held.</p>
<p>Given these challenges, senior leadership identified an opportunity to set Country Leaders up for success through a structured induction programme. This became the <b>Country Leaders Boot Camp</b>, designed to support leaders’ entry into the role at our Global Pharma client during their first 12 months.</p>
<h2>Our collaboration</h2>
<p>Our involvement began at the proposal stage, where we were excited to partner with the organisation but mindful of the potential for the programme to become too focused on information “push”.</p><p>Working closely with the client team, we co-designed a programme that placed both the business and the Country Leaders centre-stage, creating the right balance between content delivery and talent engagement. The format included two immersive three-day events at headquarters, alongside a tailored one-to-one coaching journey for each participant.</p><p>Central to the success was the deep partnership with the client team and key organisational stakeholders, who actively contributed as speakers and sponsors. Together we ensured the programme consistently put the organisation, its purpose, and its leaders first – meeting participants where they were and enabling purposeful growth.</p>
<p>What we learned:</p>
<p>While leaders need to absorb a significant amount of knowledge, focusing solely on content can miss the mark. What matters equally is how they show up. By enabling optimal readiness – tailored to each individual – we made a meaningful difference for both the individuals and the wider group.</p>
<p>We also discovered how eager participants were for feedback. Through accurate and stretching feedback, delivered in both one-to-one and group settings, we created an environment where leaders could explore and expand their leadership presence.</p>
<p>Above all, we learned the value of genuine collaboration. By letting go of assumptions, embracing uncomfortable moments, and truly listening to the needs of our target audience, we were able to go beyond the stated need and meet the real need – achieving outcomes that were transformational.</p>
<h2>Benefits &amp; Impact</h2>
<p>The programme achieved consistently high participant ratings (~9.2 in year one, ~9.3 in year two), but more importantly, it created conditions for leaders to step into their roles with greater confidence, clarity, and readiness.</p><p>Perhaps the greatest impact was helping leaders connect with themselves in ways often overlooked, setting them up not just for specific challenges, but for how they show up in every situation.</p>
