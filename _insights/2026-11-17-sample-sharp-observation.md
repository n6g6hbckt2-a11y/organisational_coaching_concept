A Sharp Observation is a short note of around 200 words: one idea about board governance, balance-sheet leadership or systemic performance, stated plainly.

To publish one, add a small text file to the `_insights` folder with a title, a date and `kind: observation` at the top, then write the note underneath in plain text. It appears on the Insights page and the home page automatically.
