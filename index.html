The intellectual spine

Both hemispheres of the boardroom.

Our work draws on three thinkers: Iain McGilchrist on integrating precise financial control with living relational context; John Vervaeke on relevance realisation, the ability to cut through noise to what matters now; and Thomas Sowell on trade-offs, incentives and the question "Compared to what?"

The result is grounded, candid, commercially realistic work, with no "coach-slop" and no "AI-slop".
