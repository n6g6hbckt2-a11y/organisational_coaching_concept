How we think

Three questions we bring to every board.

What is the whole picture?

Controls, capital and covenants on one side; trust, cohesion and judgement on the other. Value is lost when a board can only see one of them.

What actually matters now?

Boards are not short of information. They are short of attention. We help cut the agenda back to the few issues that will decide the outcome.

Compared to what?

There are no perfect options, only trade-offs. We name them plainly, cost them honestly and let the board decide with its eyes open.
