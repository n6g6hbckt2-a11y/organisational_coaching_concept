Our approach

Clear thinking first. Technology second.

Our method is simple to describe and hard to do well: see the whole system, find what matters now, and be honest about the trade-offs.

See the whole system

Boards tend to be strong on one side: controls, capital and compliance, or culture and relationships. The failures we see come from the gap between the two. A covenant breach usually starts as a conversation nobody had.

Find what matters now

Leadership teams are not short of data. They are short of attention. Much of our value is subtraction: cutting the agenda back to the two or three issues that will decide the year, and naming the one that people are avoiding.

Name the trade-off

There is no perfect option, only a choice between imperfect ones. For every proposal we ask "compared to what?", cost the alternative honestly and let the board decide with its eyes open.

How we use AI

Human in the room. Faster behind it.

We have built our own private, locally run AI tools. They are not a chatbot and they never join a session. Our conversations with clients stay human and unrecorded.

What the tools do is help us prepare. They keep a structured memory of our own notes across an engagement, so that patterns we might take months to notice are visible within weeks, and every meeting starts from where the last one finished.

Confidentiality by design

Notes from one-to-one work are held separately from team and board work, and the two never mix. Only themes that we, not the software, judge to be appropriate inform wider engagements. The approach is designed to meet the confidentiality expectations of UK and Swiss regulated clients.

How the architecture sits around the work: sessions stay human; learning flows one way into separate, ring-fenced stores; only systemic themes inform preparation.
